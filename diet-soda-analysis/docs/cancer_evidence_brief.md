# Cancer evidence brief — diet soda / aspartame (talking-point pack)

This is **not** NHANES microdata. It is the public regulatory + epidemiology context people argue about on Reddit/X, so our NHANES tests sit in the right frame.

## The claim vs the agencies (hazard ≠ risk)

| Body | Job | Aspartame bottom line (plain language) |
|------|-----|----------------------------------------|
| **IARC** (WHO cancer research arm) | **Hazard** identification | **Group 2B — “possibly carcinogenic to humans”** based on *limited* evidence in humans (discussed signal includes **liver / hepatocellular** in some ASB-era studies), *limited* animal evidence, *limited* mechanism evidence |
| **JECFA** (FAO/WHO additives committee) | **Risk** + acceptable intake | Reaffirmed **ADI 0–40 mg/kg body weight/day**; human cancer association evidence **not convincing** at usual dietary exposure |
| **FDA** | US food additive approval | Continues to allow aspartame under approved conditions; Group 2B does **not** mean “causes cancer at soda doses” |
| **NCI** | Public summary | Emphasizes limited evidence and difference between classification categories and everyday risk |

**Reddit collapse to avoid:** “WHO says diet soda causes cancer.”  
**Accurate 15-second version:** “IARC put aspartame in a *possible* hazard bin with limited evidence; the same week JECFA said stay under the ADI and did not treat usual intake as a proven cancer risk.”

### What Group 2B is (and is not)

- IARC ranks **hazard** (can it cause cancer under some conditions?), not “how dangerous is one Diet Coke.”  
- Group 2B is used when evidence is limited — it is **not** Group 1 (carcinogenic to humans).  
- **Hazard label ≠ proof that typical diet-soda use causes cancer.**

## Dose reality (ADI → cans/day)

Assumptions (order-of-magnitude; label as approximate):

- Aspartame in many diet colas often cited ~**180–200 mg per 12 oz can** (brand/formula vary).  
- JECFA ADI **40 mg/kg/day**.

| Body weight | ADI @ 40 mg/kg (mg/day) | Approx cans/day @ 200 mg/can | @ 180 mg/can |
|-------------|-------------------------|------------------------------|--------------|
| 50 kg (110 lb) | 2,000 | ~10 | ~11 |
| 60 kg (132 lb) | 2,400 | ~12 | ~13 |
| 70 kg (154 lb) | 2,800 | ~14 | ~16 |
| 80 kg (176 lb) | 3,200 | ~16 | ~18 |

See also figure `outputs/figures/cancer_c2_adi_cans.png`.

**What this does *not* mean:** “Drink 14 cans.”  
**What it does mean:** The viral “one can = cancer” slogan is **not** how risk assessors frame aspartame at labeled use.

### Reddit / viral dose folklore

| Claim style | Reality check |
|-------------|----------------|
| “Mice got cancer on aspartame” | Often at doses far above human soda use; always ask **mg/kg and human-equivalent cans**. |
| “Monkeys / 30 cans a day for years” (r/skeptic classic) | Classic **dose-scare** format; not a typical consumer pattern. |
| “Any can is poison” | Conflicts with ADI framing used by JECFA (risk assessment). |

## What big studies tend to show (benchmark — cite only)

| Study class | What Reddit hears | Reality check | Our public test |
|-------------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| **NutriNet-Santé** | Artificial sweeteners ↑ cancer | Cohort **incidence**; residual confounding debated | Cite only; cannot re-run |
| **UK Biobank / liver media** | Diet drinks → liver disease/cancer headlines | Site-specific; not all cancer | No HCC incidence in NHANES |
| **Meta-analyses ASB & mortality** | “Diet soda kills you” | Often stronger for **all-cause/CVD** than **cancer mortality** | Cox cancer death: null, underpowered |
| **Rodent high-dose** | “Science proved cancer” | Dose translation fails | ADI cans chart |

## What NHANES can and cannot do for cancer

| Can | Cannot |
|-----|--------|
| Self-reported **ever cancer** (MCQ220) by beverage group | Incident cancer with latency |
| **Cancer death** via public Linked Mortality File | Site-specific HCC incidence |
| Age-stratify crude scares | Lifetime usual diet (24h recall only) |
| Show **who** drinks diet soda (confounding) | Prove chemical causation |

## Verdict language (locked)

- **BUSTED as a slogan:** “Diet soda gives you cancer” as a simple certainty.  
- **UNTESTABLE HERE:** low-HR long-latency site-specific cancer (e.g. liver) from one public survey.  
- **NUANCED:** crude ever-cancer higher in diet-soda drinkers partly because they are **older / more comorbid**; residual association possible.  
- **Never:** “We proved aspartame is safe forever.”
